The first release: Atari 2600 *Haunted House* running unmodified in the Stella emulator, redrawn live from
its RAM as an NES game or a Super NES one, with the original alongside.

- Two looks, each with its own pack and soundtrack: NES (chiptune) and SNES (orchestra).
- The house is alive: flickering candles, portraits whose eyes follow you, a storm with lightning and thunder,
  rain and creaking floorboards where the music stops. M turns the music off and leaves the house's sounds.
- The endings: dawn over the house when you escape with the urn, the window going dark when you don't.
- V cycles the views, up to the four-way grid: your game, the other look, the 2600 and its live RAM.
- R records gameplay (needs ffmpeg); S takes a screenshot.

You need your own Haunted House ROM; the game asks for it the first time and remembers it.
README.txt in the download explains how to open the app the first time (it isn't signed).

**Downloads:** Windows (x64) and macOS (Apple silicon). SHA256SUMS.txt has their checksums.
