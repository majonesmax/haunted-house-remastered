Help for finding your way through the house - and a few fixes found by playing it.

- **G gives a hint:** how many pieces of the urn are left and which way the nearest is (up or down the
  stairs, or which way across the floor), how to take it once you're in its room, and with the whole urn,
  which way to the front door. It points; it never marks anything on screen.
- **O turns the lights on** - a cheat, for when you just want to see where everything is. You still need a
  lit match to pick anything up.
- **Urn pieces you can find:** the urn is now broken across (lip, body, foot) instead of down its middle,
  where two of the pieces were just a tiny handle; and every piece glints now and then.
- **The game says what the original's manual did:** you take things by walking into them with a match lit,
  and when the urn comes together, a reminder that it isn't won until it's out of the front door.
- **Fixed:** losing your last life while holding the whole urn showed the win's ending.
- **Fixed:** the sound stuttered while walking out of the front door (the NES look's fade to white).
