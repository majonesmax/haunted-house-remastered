Three things found by playing - and by watching the playthrough video.

- **"You put down the urn to take the scepter."** You hold one thing at a time, and joined pieces are one
  thing - the urn so far - so picking up something else swaps them. The original never says so; now a
  message does, the moment it happens.
- **The status bar says what it shows:** HOLD over what's in your hand, and URN over the three slots, which
  show how much of the urn is mended - not three things you're carrying.
- **The ending:** after the escape, the eyes no longer walk on past the end of the hill into the sky; they
  stop on the grass with the urn, facing the morning.
