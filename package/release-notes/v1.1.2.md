Two small fixes to how the picture is shown.

- **The 2600 picture is no longer stretched.** Beside the remaster (and alone), its 228 lines are each shown
  once, at the TV's shape, with a thin bar above and below. Before, they were stretched to 240 lines, so one
  line in about 19 was drawn twice.
- **No tearing:** the window uses vsync where the system offers it. On a ~60 Hz screen, the screen's refresh
  paces the game; on others (a 120 Hz screen, say) the game's own clock does, as before.
